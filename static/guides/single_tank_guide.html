Single-Tank Calculator — Quick Start

Size one AquaCell system end-to-end: enter your project and tank geometry, get storage volume, bill of materials, load verification, and a ready-to-export report.

This is the main calculator — it's what loads by default. Every result is a preliminary, conceptual estimate; the Engineer of Record is responsible for final design and verification. The form runs top to bottom in six collapsible cards on the left; results build on the right as soon as you click Calculate.

Card 1

Project Information

Project name, number, location, client, and estimator — this is what shows up on every PDF you export. Below it, Project Notes / Special Instructions / Assumptions is a free-text field that prints straight onto the report.

Card 2

Installation Parameters

  1. Pick Configuration (Standard or Extra Strong) and Number of Layers, and set a Minimum Required Storage Volume if the project has one to check against.
  2. Choose a Tank Shape Mode: ▭ Rectangle for a simple known width × length, or ⬠ Complex Shape for an irregular footprint — enter a scaled area plus one known dimension, and the tool derives the rest.
  3. Either way, enter the values as scaled or measured off the plans — the calculator snaps them down to the nearest whole crate module automatically.

In Complex Shape mode, an optional Void Space Entry section lets you carve out obstructions inside the footprint — concrete islands, light poles, monuments — that the tank has to form around. Each void's area comes out of the tank footprint and its perimeter is added back in; the void itself earns no storage credit.

Cards 3 & 4

Site Conditions & Backfill

  1. Elevations & Traffic — surface elevation, tank bottom elevation, and Traffic Load (H⁠-⁠10, HS⁠-⁠20, or HS⁠-⁠25). This drives the minimum cover check in Cover & Load Verification.
  2. Installation / Backfill Requirements — perimeter, cover, and base stone depths, stone void ratio, and geotextile waste/overlap %. Defaults are typical values; adjust to match your spec.
Cards 5 & 6

Accessories & Report Options

  1. Accessories — pipe connectors and top adapters auto-suggest a quantity once you calculate; override any of them by typing over the suggestion. Contingency Base Units rounds up to a full pallet automatically.
  2. Report Options — toggle whether the PDF includes the schematic layout and the stage storage table, and set the Stage Storage Increment (1–12 inches) for that table.
Run it

Calculate & Read the Results

Click ▶ Calculate in the bottom bar (or anywhere the button appears). The right-hand panel and the quick-glance dashboard at the top both fill in immediately.

💧 Storage Summary 📦 Bill of Materials 🪨 Stone Backfill 🧵 Geotextile & Geogrid 🛡 Cover & Load Verification 📐 Excavation Summary 📈 Stage Storage Table 💲 Price Estimation

Three things worth knowing

  1. 💲 Price Estimation is a second, separate step. Calculate builds the system and its Bill of Materials; pricing doesn't happen automatically. Set Markup % and Freight % in the Price Estimation card at the top of the results, then click its own Calculate Price button to get Calculated Cost, Selling Price, Est. Freight, and Total to Customer. Run Calculate first — Calculate Price reads the BOM quantities Calculate already produced.
  2. 🔵 PT⁠-⁠ROW™ Pre⁠-⁠Treatment Sizing is off by default — check Enable PT⁠-⁠ROW™ in that result card's header if the project needs it.
  3. Scroll down on the right for the Conceptual Plan View + Section Schematic — an auto-generated plan and cross-section drawing of the tank, stone envelope, and cover.
Bottom bar

Exporting Your Work

  1. 📄 Quote — the client-facing quote PDF. Run Calculate and then Calculate Price first — Quote does not check for you, and skipping Calculate Price will silently produce a PDF with $0 pricing instead of a warning.
  2. ⬇ Report — the internal design summary PDF: full inputs, storage/BOM/load results, and the schematic if included. No pricing on it — that's what Quote is for.
  3. ⬇ DXF — a computed module-grid CAD layout: plan view with dimensions, plus a cross-section view if Estimated Surface Elevation and AquaCell Tank Bottom Elevation are both entered. Rectangular tanks only — not available for Complex Shape layouts. This is a conceptual drawing, not a stamped construction drawing; native AquaCell DWG detail sheets remain the source of truth for module, plate, and connector geometry.
  4. 💲 CS Export — a per-line-item unit-cost CSV built from the same floor prices as Quote, for anyone who needs the cost breakdown outside the tool.
  5. 📊 Stage CSV / 📈 Stage PNG — the stage⁠-⁠storage table as a spreadsheet, or the storage curve as an image, for detention routing work outside the tool.
  6. 📋 Details — opens the AquaCell detail sheet library to attach standard details to your submittal.
  7. 📋 Copy — copies a plain-text summary of the results to your clipboard, ready to paste into an email.

Use Export (left panel, below Reset Form) to save the entire session as a .json file, and Import to reload it later — handy for picking a project back up, or handing it to a colleague. 🆕 New Project in the top bar clears everything and starts fresh.
